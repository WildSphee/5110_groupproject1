# Suggested analyses in the 2025 dashboard

| Suggested topic | Page and scope |
|---|---|
| Stage vignette, ITT routes and longest day | 02 shows the selected route, stage type, distance, date, recorded winner and top ten. Use the 21 stage buttons. |
| Team contribution and depth | 01 compares all completed rider-hours and roster counts. 04 explains the fastest-three team classification. |
| Awards and mountain performance | 03 compares the supported jerseys and separates alternative age and mountain-time ranks. 02 shows mountain podiums, terrain speed and top-ten finishes by terrain. |
| Participation and national outcomes | 01 pairs a country map with country counts, a filtered rider roster, best GC rank, wins and podiums. |
| GC turning points and time adjustments | 05 shows gaps and daily rank movement. 06 identifies the last GC rank before exit. 07 compares four timing scenarios and each exited rider's remaining time budget. |

Page choices and the limits of the 2025 source are documented in the [Task 3 matrix](requirements-matrix.md).
