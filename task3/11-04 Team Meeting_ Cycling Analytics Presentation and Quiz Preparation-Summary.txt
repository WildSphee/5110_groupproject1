## Rider Exit and Withdrawal Analysis
The exits dataset contains 24 rider exits. The analysis should distinguish **DNS** (Did Not Start: a rider completed the previous stage but did not start the next) from a **mid-stage withdrawal**. The team aims to identify meaningful patterns, including the proportion of exits during mountain stages—reported as half of all exits—riders’ GC positions before leaving, including cases where riders in third place withdrew, and the explicit reasons recorded in the populated exit table.
